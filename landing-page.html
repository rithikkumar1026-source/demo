03FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about ordering, delivery and our menu.

Do you offer home delivery?

Yes. You can order our food for delivery on Zomato or Swiggy. Open the Order & Pickup section to choose one.

Can I order ahead and pick up?

Yes. Add your items with the + button in the menu and send the order on WhatsApp, or call us. We will have it ready for you to collect at the counter.

How often does the menu change?

We change part of our menu every two weeks, so there is always something new. The menu section on this page shows what is on right now.

Do you have vegetarian options?

Yes. Every category has vegetarian dishes. They are marked with a green dot, and non-vegetarian dishes have a red dot.

Are your fruit shakes and juices really fresh?

Yes. We blend or press them when you order, using real fruit and milk.

Do you take bulk or party orders?

Yes. Message us on WhatsApp with the date and the items you need, and we will confirm if we can do it.
